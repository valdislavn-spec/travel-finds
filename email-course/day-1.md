# Day 1 — Why flexible dates save the most

Subject: The #1 trick that cuts US flight prices (no points needed)
Preheader: Same route, different day — up to 40% cheaper.

## Body
Hi {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "there" }},

Most people book a flight for the exact date they want. Airlines know it — and charge for it.

**The trick:** shift your search by ±1–2 days. Tuesday/Wednesday departures are routinely $60–$150 cheaper than Friday/Sunday on the same route.

Tonight, do this:
1. Pick one route from your home airport.
2. Search the exact dates.
3. Search again ±2 days.
4. Compare the totals — not the base fare (baggage and seat fees change the math).
