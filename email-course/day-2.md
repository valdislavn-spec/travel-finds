Rule of thumb from recent fare data:

- **Long-haul international** (Europe, Asia): 2–4 months before departure.
- **Domestic US**: 4–8 weeks before.
- **Holidays**: 4–6 months — and avoid the exact peak days.

A fare is usually cheapest in the first weeks after airlines load schedules, then rises as the cabin fills. Last-minute deals exist, but they're the exception.
