A $300 fare can turn into a $600 trip. Budget for:
- **Baggage** — check the airline's carry-on rules before booking.
- **eSIM** — usually $5–$20 for a trip; roaming can be 10x more.
- **Transfers** — airport train/bus vs taxi; book ahead for fixed prices.
- **Travel insurance** — optional but worth it for long-haul.
