Subject: The airport 40 minutes away can save you $200
Preheader: Same city, different airport, very different price.

## Body
Hi {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "there" }},

Big hubs aren't always cheapest. Check:
- **New York:** JFK vs EWR vs nearby PHL
- **Los Angeles:** LAX vs BUR vs ONT
- **San Francisco:** SFO vs OAK vs SJC
- **Chicago:** ORD vs MDW

Also consider a positioning flight: a cheap domestic hop to a major gateway can beat an expensive direct long-haul — but leave a buffer of 4+ hours and book on one ticket where possible.
