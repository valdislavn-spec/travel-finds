# Day 3 — Error fares: real but rare

Subject: How to catch a mistake fare before it disappears
Preheader: $180 to Europe is real — but it lasts hours.

## Body
Hi {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "there" }},

Sometimes an airline publishes a wrong price — a "mistake fare". They usually last 2–12 hours.

How to catch them:
- Turn on push/email alerts for your hub.
- Check fares early morning (US time) — mistakes often appear overnight.
- Book directly with the airline when possible; skip add-ons until ticketed.
- Pay with a card that has trip protection.

Do not book non-refundable hotels before the ticket is confirmed.
