How to book cheaper

  • Shift dates by ±1 day. Mid-week departures are often $60–$150 cheaper than Fri/Sun.
  • Book the long haul 2–4 months ahead for international, 4–8 weeks for domestic.
  • Check secondary airports (Burbank, Newark, Oakland) — same city, lower fees.
  • Use a fare calendar to find the cheapest week, then book directly.
  • Bundle eSIM and transfers — small savings that add up.
  • Set price alerts and buy when the fare drops below your target.
