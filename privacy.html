Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Applies to the affiliate-fanout tool and its OAuth integrations.

affiliate-fanout is an open-source tool that publishes travel deal posts containing affiliate links to social platforms on behalf of its operator. This policy explains what data is processed.

1. Data we process

  • Content you publish: post text, titles and target URLs that you provide to the tool.
  • Platform identifiers: account/page IDs and OAuth tokens returned by the platforms you connect (X, Meta, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Telegram and others). Tokens are stored locally in your environment (.env) and are never sent to us or to third parties.
  • Aggregate click statistics: the built-in link tracker records click counts and referrer platform labels for affiliate links. It does not collect names, emails or other personal data.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not operate user accounts, do not collect emails/phone numbers, and do not sell or share data. No advertising SDKs or tracking cookies are used on this page.

3. Third-party platforms

When you publish, content is sent to third-party platforms (e.g. X, Meta/Facebook/Instagram/Threads, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Telegram, Tumblr, Bluesky, Mastodon) under their own terms and privacy policies. Affiliate links may be redirected through Travelpayouts and the operator's link tracker for attribution.

4. Affiliate disclosure

Published posts contain affiliate links. We disclose this in the post (e.g. “Ad · affiliate link” / “Publicidad · enlace de afiliado”). We may receive a commission when someone books through these links, at no extra cost to the user.

5. Security & retention

OAuth tokens are kept in local environment files with restricted permissions; click statistics are aggregated and retained for reporting only. Remove the app from your platform account at any time to revoke access.

6. Contact

Questions or data requests: open an issue.
